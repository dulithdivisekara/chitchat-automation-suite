# Stranger Chat Texting Etiquette & Language Guide (English)

## Why Stranger Chat Language Is Different
On random chat platforms (Omegle, Chitchat.gg, Chatroulette), conversation is fast-paced. People skip in 2 to 3 seconds if messages look:
- **Too formal or long** (looks like an AI bot, scammer, or marketer)
- **Too slow** (people lose interest immediately)

---

## 1. The Screening Phase (First 5 Seconds)

| What Strangers Type | Meaning | How to Respond (Natural) |
|---|---|---|
| `m` / `male` / `18m` | Boy | Skip immediately! (90% of chat users are male) |
| `f` / `female` / `19f` | Girl | Match found! Break the ice immediately. |
| `u?` / `wbu?` / `m or f?` | Asking your gender | `20m u?` or `m here, u?` (always lowercase) |
| `hi` / `hey` / `sup` | Casual greeting | `heyy, f or m?` |

## 3. Natural Flow When a Girl Is Found

### Phase 1: The Icebreaker (First 1-2 Messages)
Keep it short, relaxed, and casual:
- `"heyy, where u from?"`
- `"hey :) how's your night going?"`
- `"cool haha, wyd tonight?"`

### Phase 2: Connecting Over Basic Topics
- **Location:**
  - If she says a country/city: *"nice! heard it's really pretty there, u live there long?"*
- **Activities / Boredom:**
  - If she says *"bored"*: *"haha same, scrolling on here to pass time. what do u usually do for fun?"*
  - If she says music/movies: *"what kind of music u into lately?"*

### Key Rules for Texting as a Guy:
1. **Never capitalize every letter** (don't write *"Hello Where Are You From"* -> write *"hey where u from"*).
2. **Never send huge walls of text** (keep messages to 1 to 2 lines max).
3. **Use friendly punctuation** (e.g. `:)`, `haha`, `lol`).
4. **Always ask an easy question back** so the conversation doesn't stall.
